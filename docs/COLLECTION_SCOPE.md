# Collection scope

## Archive principle

Collect official source facts and provenance broadly enough to support downstream analysis without embedding analytical judgments in this repository.

## Council scopes

### cheongju_integrated
Initial target:
- terms 1-4
- official meeting records
- current and historical member rosters
- official profile portrait where available
- tenure / party / district / committee history
- bills and resolutions
- official activity/source links

### cheongju_old_city
Target:
- historically published member rosters and representative portrait where available
- officially available meeting records and metadata
- historical term numbering kept independent from integrated council terms

### cheongwon_old_county
Target:
- historically published member rosters and representative portrait where available
- officially available meeting records and metadata
- historical term numbering kept independent from integrated council terms

## Binary policy

Mirror:
- meeting text in normalized text form
- one official profile portrait per person

Do not mirror by default:
- meeting attachments
- PDFs / HWP / HWPX and other large source files
- video

Retain official URL and source identifiers instead.

## Version policy

Meeting text:
- provisional: retain
- published: preferred current analysis source
- superseded: retain as historical version with successor linkage

Member attributes:
- never overwrite history with current affiliation
- preserve effective period or the narrowest source-supported precision
